Lesson 0017 — ICU, APU and the floor walker

Primary source: your TTEC experience (ICU/APU) + onboarding SME practice.

Why this lesson

Performance management in a centre is not a single event — it's a staged escalation. ICU (intensive care) and APU (action plan) are two units for persistently low-performing agents, each with defined duration and exit criteria. The floor walker (onboarding SME) is the first line of prevention.

Concept

  • Floor walker / onboarding SME: runs live onboarding (Day 0 → graduation), coaches daily, sets objectives, runs occasional written reviews.
  • ICU: six weeks of close quality supervision and coaching for persistently low performers.
  • APU: close monitoring against a specific KPI after four weeks of coaching, ahead of a final decision.
  • Escalation ladder: coaching → ICU → APU → final decision. Each rung has duration and criteria.

You ran solo onboarding for up to 21 concurrent waves (~15 agents each), including sole onboarding for the India site. Recognised by India team and Vodafone UK leadership for quality and consistency at that scale.
