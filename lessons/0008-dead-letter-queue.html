Lesson 0008 — Dead letter queue (DLQ)

Primary source: Enterprise Integration Patterns — Dead Letter Channel.

Why this lesson

When a message or submission cannot be processed (bad schema, failed gate, unknown role), you have two bad options: drop it (data loss) or retry forever (poison pill). A dead letter queue is the third option: park it, record why, and let a human decide.

Concept

  • DLQ: a separate queue/channel where unprocessable messages land.
  • Reason recorded: why it failed (validation error, gate verdict, timeout) — not just "failed".
  • Human triage: DLQs are worked by operators, not auto-retried blindly.
  • Retry policy: limited retries with backoff before DLQ; never infinite.

Helix's gate returns dead_letter for unprocessable submissions (unknown role, forbidden classification, non-owned engine, oversight-only seat) — the submission is held and recorded, never executed, never silently dropped.
