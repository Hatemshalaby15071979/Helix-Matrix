Lesson 0007 — Correlation IDs

Primary source: W3C Trace Context.

Why this lesson

When a request crosses services (API → gate → engine → audit), a single identifier ties the whole journey together. Without it, "why did this fail?" becomes a grep across five logs.

Concept

  • Correlation ID: a unique id generated at the edge and propagated through every hop.
  • Propagation: passed in a header (X-Correlation-ID / W3C traceparent) and logged at each service.
  • One id, many logs: grep the id → the entire request's story across services.
  • Per-tenant scoping: combine with tenant id for safe, isolated tracing.

Helix attaches per-request correlation IDs in its tenant-isolated async ingest layer; CompliProof's hash-chained audit trail uses them to link a reconciliation to its evidence.
