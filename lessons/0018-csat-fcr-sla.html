Lesson 0018 — CSAT, FCR and SLA

Primary source: contact-centre KPI standard definitions + your experience.

Why this lesson

Three customer-outcome metrics that every centre tracks, and that are often confused or gamed. Knowing what each measures (and what it doesn't) is the difference between managing outcomes and managing numbers.

Concept

  • CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): post-contact survey score. Measures perceived experience. Lagging, biased by who responds.
  • FCR (First Contact Resolution): % of contacts resolved without a follow-up. Measures efficiency and quality together. Hard to measure honestly (requires a time window and a definition of "resolved").
  • SLA (Service Level Agreement): contractual promise, usually expressed as SL (e.g. 80% in 20s). Measures access, not quality.

A centre can hit SLA while CSAT tanks (fast but bad answers) and FCR stays low (repeat contacts). The three must be read together.
