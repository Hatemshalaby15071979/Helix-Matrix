Lesson 0010 — KPI-decay score

Primary source: Helix CX Churn Sentinel + your RTA Command Center 5-stage pipeline.

Why this lesson

A single KPI snapshot (CSAT 82%) is a photograph. A KPI-decay score is a video: it tracks how a metric trends over time and turns "drifting but still green" into an early-warning signal before SLA breach.

Concept

  • Decay: a metric's distance from target, weighted by recency and rate of change.
  • Threshold bands: green / amber / red, loaded from config (not hardcoded) — single source of truth.
  • Pipeline: raw KPI → normalise → weight → decay → classify → route to action.
  • Honest about limits: a heuristic scorer with configurable thresholds is not a prediction; label it.

RTA Command Center: five-stage KPI pipeline turning CX KPIs into an account-risk signal, later absorbed into Helix's RTA engine. Helix's CX risk scorer loads kpi_weights/kpi_thresholds/risk_bands from config/risk_thresholds.yaml (was hardcoded — fixed).
