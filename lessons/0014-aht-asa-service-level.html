Lesson 0014 — AHT, ASA and service level

Primary source: contact-centre WFM standard metrics + Helix WFM engine.

Why this lesson

Three metrics every WFM professional lives by: AHT (how long a contact takes), ASA (how long a customer waits), and service level (what fraction is answered within target). Confusing them is the most common WFM error.

Concept

  • AHT (Average Handle Time): talk + hold + after-call work, averaged. Drives traffic intensity (A = volume × AHT).
  • ASA (Average Speed of Answer): average wait time for answered calls. ASA = Pw · AHT / (N − A).
  • Service Level (SL): % of calls answered within target time T. SL(t) = 1 − Pw·e^(-(N-A)·t/AHT).
  • Occupancy: % of time agents are busy (talking + ACW). High occupancy → burnout; too low → waste.

Helix's WFM engine computes SL with a documented 20s answer threshold and ASA = C·AHT/(N−A); the fake flat 0.05*agents confidence band was removed as dishonest.
