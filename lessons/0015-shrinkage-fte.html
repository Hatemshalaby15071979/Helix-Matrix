Lesson 0015 — Shrinkage and FTE

Primary source: standard WFM practice; Helix WFM engine.

Why this lesson

Raw agent counts from Erlang C are not enough: agents are not available 100% of the time (breaks, training, meetings, absence). Shrinkage converts required agents into required FTE. Forgetting shrinkage is the #1 staffing shortfall.

Concept

  • Shrinkage: % of paid time an agent is unavailable for contact handling (breaks, training, meetings, absence, admin).
  • FTE required: agents_needed / (1 − shrinkage).
  • Typical shrinkage: 25–35% in contact centres (varies by site, shift, season).
  • Net staffing: scheduled agents = FTE × (1 − shrinkage) after all deductions.

Helix's WFM adapter takes shrinkage as an input and computes FTE; the B2B onboarding engine generates staffing plans from workload data.
