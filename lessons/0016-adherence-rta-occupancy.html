Lesson 0016 — Adherence, RTA and occupancy

Primary source: Helix RTA engine + RTA Command Center (5-stage KPI pipeline).

Why this lesson

Adherence (did the agent follow the schedule?) and RTA (real-time analysis) are the bridge between planning and execution. Occupancy measures how busy agents are. Together they tell you whether the plan matched reality — and where to intervene.

Concept

  • Adherence: % of scheduled time an agent was in the right state at the right time.
  • RTA (Real-Time Analysis): live monitoring of adherence, occupancy, queue, SL vs. plan; triggers coaching/escalation.
  • Occupancy: (talk + ACW) / (talk + ACW + available). High sustained occupancy = burnout risk.
  • Shrinkage vs. occupancy: shrinkage is planned unavailability; occupancy is actual busyness while available.

RTA Command Center: five-stage KPI pipeline turning CX KPIs into an account-risk signal, later absorbed into Helix's RTA engine. Weighted heuristic risk scorer with configurable thresholds.
